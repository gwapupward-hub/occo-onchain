# OCCO — OnChain Credit Organization

## Overview
OCCO is a data and analytics provider offering standardized credit scores for on-chain wallets.

OCCO does not issue credit, make lending decisions, or provide financial advice.

## Problem
On-chain financial markets lack standardized credit intelligence. Risk assessment is fragmented and inconsistent.

## Solution
OCCO aggregates public blockchain data and produces deterministic, explainable credit scores for wallets.

## Product
- OCCO Score (300–850)
- Risk Tiers (A–E)
- Credit Reports
- Verifiable attestations

## Methodology
- Public on-chain data only
- Deterministic scoring models
- Versioned and auditable

## Compliance Positioning
- OCCO is a data and analytics provider
- OCCO does not collect PII by default
- Scores are informational analytics and subject to model limitations

## Customers
- DeFi protocols
- Marketplaces
- Risk teams
- Compliance teams

## Business Model
- API subscriptions
- Enterprise integrations
- Reporting services
